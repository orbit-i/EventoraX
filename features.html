EventoriaX Features

Our innovative, future‑ready features make your event management effortless — ensuring a seamless experience without any hurdles.

01

Event Management

Create, organize and manage events of every size with powerful tools and automation.

→

What you'll get

Create, organize, and manage every stage of your event from a single dashboard. Schedule physical, online, or hybrid events, customize registration settings, define attendance limits, assign certificate templates, and monitor event progress in real time. Whether you're hosting a workshop, conference, seminar, or university event, EventoriaX helps you streamline planning while keeping all event information organized and accessible.

02

Certificate System

Design, issue and verify professional certificates in seconds with automation.

→

What you'll get

Generate professional digital certificates instantly with customizable templates designed for every type of event. Issue certificates individually or in bulk, include organization branding, and send them directly to participants via email. Every certificate contains a unique verification code and SHA-256 security hash, allowing anyone to verify its authenticity through the public verification page.

03

QR Ticketing & Scanner

Generate secure QR tickets and scan attendees instantly using any device.

→

What you'll get

Automatically generate secure QR tickets for every registered attendee and simplify event check-in with fast, camera-based scanning. Track ticket usage in real time, prevent duplicate entries, and manage attendance without paperwork. Tickets can also be downloaded in bulk or delivered directly through automated email, making event entry faster and more reliable.

04

ID Card Generator

Create professional ID cards for participants, speakers and staff in minutes.

→

What you'll get

Create professional ID cards for participants, speakers, volunteers, and staff with just a few clicks. Select from multiple templates, customize logos and displayed information, preview designs before publishing, and export high-quality PDFs individually or in bulk. This ensures a consistent and professional identity for everyone attending your event.

05

Analytics & Reports

Get clear insight into registerations, attendance, certificates and event performance.

→

What you'll get

Gain valuable insights into your events with detailed analytics and visual reports. Monitor registrations, attendance rates, certificate issuance, event performance, and participation trends through interactive dashboards. Export reports in PDF format to support decision-making, measure event success, and improve future planning with data-driven insights.

06

Email Automation

Sent the right emails at the right time with automation and smart triggers.

→

What you'll get

Keep participants informed with automated email workflows that deliver the right message at the right time. Send registration confirmations, event reminders, QR tickets, digital certificates, renewal notices, and important updates without manual effort. Built-in email automation improves communication while saving valuable administrative time.

07

Reset API

Integrate EventoriaX with your own systems using our powerful Reset API.

→

What you'll get

Connect EventoriaX with your existing applications using secure REST API access available for Enterprise organizations. Generate and manage API tokens, integrate with third-party systems, automate workflows, and exchange event data securely through a documented API, making EventoriaX part of your existing technology ecosystem.

08

Multi-tenancy & security

Built for scale isolated, secure and reliable for every organization.

→

What you'll get

Built with a secure multi-tenant architecture, EventoriaX ensures every organization operates within its own isolated workspace while sharing a reliable cloud platform. Role-based permissions, activity logs, secure authentication, audit tracking, and certificate verification protect sensitive information and provide complete transparency for administrators and organizations of every size.
